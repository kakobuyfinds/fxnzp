What to check before ordering

Confirm the variant

Save the seller link and selected color. Compare the seller’s garment measurements with a similar item you own. For shoes, compare foot length with the listing’s size chart.

Review warehouse photos

Match the label and color to your order. Ask for a photo showing the tape measure if fit depends on a specific dimension. Check the return deadline before deciding whether to ship.

Compare total cost

Add the item price, domestic freight, agent fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Ask whether the shipping quote uses actual weight or dimensional weight.
